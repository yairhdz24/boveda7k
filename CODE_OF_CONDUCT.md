# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language, or publishing someone's private information.
- Spanish and English are both welcome. Be patient with people writing in their second language.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes the project unwelcoming, report it privately through the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, marking it as a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential and reviewed by the maintainer.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently for behavior they consider inappropriate or harmful.
